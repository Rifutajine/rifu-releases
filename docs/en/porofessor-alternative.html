What RIFU adds

In champ select, RIFU explains what your comp is missing and suggests three champions from your pool, each with its reason: “the only one with frontline”, “won 7 times out of 10”. You always have the final say.

  • Draft advice based on the comp's gaps, never “play X”
  • Post-game score out of 100, weighted for your role
  • Recurring mistakes tracked from one game to the next
  • Today's LP and a 30-day curve
  • Skin collection with its RP value

Pick RIFU if…

You want help in champ select, a review that explains your score and LP tracking, in a single free app, without Overwolf.

Stay on Porofessor if…

You play on a server other than EUW, or don't want to join a beta: RIFU is in closed, invite-only beta and covers EUW only for now.

Frequently asked questions

Every other answer is on the home page.

Does RIFU scout the loading screen like Porofessor?

Yes. On the loading screen, RIFU shows all ten players with their rank, mastery, form over 12 hours and 30 days, their premade groups and tags such as “On a roll” or “One-trick”, with the team game plan on top.

What's the real difference with Porofessor?

Champion select. RIFU reads what your comp is missing (frontline, engage, magic damage…) and suggests three champions from your own pool, each with its reason. It also adds LP tracking, progress from one game to the next and a skin collection.

Do I need Overwolf to use RIFU?

No. RIFU is a standalone Windows app: nothing else to install.

Is RIFU free?

Yes, completely, with no subscription. Eventually a discreet ad will appear in the RIFU window, never in champ select, on the loading screen, in game or in an overlay.
