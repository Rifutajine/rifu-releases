Every RIFU feature, screen by screen

From champion select to the post-game review, then between games: what RIFU does, where to find it, and what it will never do.

Champion select

RIFU follows the draft live and tells you what each champion in your pool brings that the others don’t. You always have the final say.

  • Three options from your pool, no imposed ranking
  • A reason for each: “The only one with frontline”, “7 wins in 11”
  • Type any champion: RIFU rates it next to the others
  • Suggested bans and an “us versus them” comp, axis by axis
  • Champion locked: runes, spells and item set with “Import all”

Loading screen

A full screen over League's loading screen, with the game plan on top. Rank, form, premades: you know who you're facing before the first second.

  • Team game plan and your personal matchup memo at the top
  • Premades spelled out: “Duo with Poing”, “Trio with…”
  • Rank, mastery, 12 h / 30 d activity and tags (“On a roll”, “One-trick”)
  • Ranks show first, full cards follow
  • Reminder of your recurring mistake on the champion you play, with its tip

In-game overlays

A compact HUD that lets your clicks through, the Ctrl + X panel and item gaps on TAB. Three presets: Minimal, Standard, Full.

  • Next buy adapted to the enemy comp without weakening your champion
  • Ctrl + X: one row per lane, each player facing their opponent
  • Compact HUD: CS/min against your lane opponent, kill participation, vision per minute
  • Dragon and Baron timers on the minimap
  • Team gold and item gap while holding TAB
  • Opacity from 30 to 100%, size from 80 to 130%

Post-game review

Every stat is compared with the best of the ten players, weighted for your role. A support is judged on vision, not minions.

  • Score out of 100 weighted by role, MVP status and LP won or lost
  • What worked, what didn’t: strengths and things to work on, with numbers
  • Four tabs (Summary, Timeline, Comparison, Players) and a shareable card
  • The moment the game turned, your game phases and your lane duel
  • Your game against your average, and the records you beat

Progress and recurring mistakes

RIFU reviews every game and remembers what keeps happening. You work on one thing at a time and see when it’s fixed, always compared with your own level.

  • A mistake flagged when it comes back from one game to the next
  • A suggested goal, such as “at most 1 death before 15 min”, that you take or not
  • A summary of your week on the home screen, from 5 games
  • Per-champion review from 3 reviewed games
  • Farming, fighting, vision, survival and objectives, with your 30-day LP

ARAM, ARAM Mayhem and Swiftplay

The advice changes with the mode. In ARAM, roles stop mattering: the bench and what the team lacks do.

  • What your team lacks: tank, engage, poke, healing
  • Your champion compared with the bench and your cards
  • Mode-specific builds, runes and spells, health relic
  • In Mayhem, the augments most picked with your champion (never their win rate)
  • Swiftplay: builds for both your champions side by side, from the lobby

Supported modes

Summoner's Rift (normals, ranked, Co-op vs AI, custom games), Swiftplay, ARAM and ARAM Mayhem. In Arena, RIFU gives no draft advice, and Brawl games aren't tracked. You can turn overlays off mode by mode.

Tier list and skin collection

Top-three podium, S+ to D tiers (S+ reserved for the five best in the role) and a “My pool” filter, computed by RIFU from EUW Emerald+ ranked games. On the collection side: your skins against the total, completion, store value in RP and your chromas, kept on your PC.

Everyday tools

One-click runes and items
Rune page, spells on the right key and item set in the shop, together or separately. Your own sets are kept.
Draft simulator
Test a comp outside a game and see what your pool doesn't cover.
Matchup memo
A memo against each opponent, recalled at the next draft and loading screen. It stays on your PC.
LP tracking
Today's LP on the home screen, a 30-day curve, even for games played with RIFU closed.
Player profiles
Ranks, tags and champions of any EUW player, and every game in their history scored out of 100.
This patch for you
A Buff, Nerf or Adjusted chip next to your champions and items, every patch.
Champion pages
Build, runes, abilities and matchups for every champion, outside a game, with your own record on them.
Always on patch
Items, runes and abilities update themselves every patch, with no new version.
Match found
Sound alert, and auto-accept if you turn it on.
Daily challenge
Three League trivia questions on the home screen, the same for everyone.
Ctrl + K
Jump to any screen from the keyboard. Try it on this site.

What RIFU will never do

RIFU follows Riot's rules for third-party apps. What follows isn't an oversight, it's a choice.

  • Read the game's memory

    Official APIs only. RIFU only touches League's files to enable replays, when you ask.

  • Tell you what to play

    Always several options, from your pool, each with its reason.

  • Give a hidden advantage

    No MMR estimate, no unmasking of hidden players in champ select, no tracking of enemy summoner spells.

  • Send your data for no reason

    Match history, memos and settings stay on your PC. Details in the privacy policy.
