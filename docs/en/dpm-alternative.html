Advice, never orders

RIFU doesn't tell you what to play and shows no win probability in champ select. It shows what the comp is missing and three options from your pool, each with its reason.

  • Three options from your pool, no imposed ranking
  • Score out of 100, compared with the ten players of the game
  • Recurring mistakes tracked from one game to the next
  • ARAM and ARAM Mayhem: what the team lacks, mode-specific builds

Pick RIFU if…

You want champ select help, overlays and LP tracking without paying a subscription, in an app that follows Riot's rules for third-party apps.

Keep DPM if…

You rely on background clip recording, or play on a server other than EUW: RIFU is in closed beta, on EUW for now.

Frequently asked questions

Every other answer is on the home page.

Is DPM's Windows app paid?

According to its public offer as of October 2026, DPM's Windows app requires the Premium subscription (€4.99 a month, €24.99 for six months or €39.99 a year). The dpm.lol website stays free with ads.

Does RIFU show a win probability in champ select?

No, by design. RIFU always offers several options, each with its reason, and lets you decide. It estimates neither MMR nor your chances of winning a draft.

Does RIFU record clips of my games?

No. RIFU opens the League client's replays, but doesn't record video in the background.

Is RIFU free?

Yes, completely and with no subscription: draft advice, overlays, post-game review and LP tracking included.
