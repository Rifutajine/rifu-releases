Real-time game flow

Four moments, one companion

From champ select to the post-game, RIFU switches screens along with League. Hover the highlights or click the numbered pins to inspect each screen.

1Champ select 2Loading 3In game 4Post-game
01 · Champion Select

Your options, each with its reason

RIFU follows the draft live and tells you what each champion in your pool brings that the others don't. You always have the final say.

  • Three options from your pool, with no forced ranking
  • A reason of its own for each: “The only one with frontline”, “7 wins in 11”
  • Type any champion: RIFU rates it alongside the others
  • Suggested bans and an “us vs. them” comp view, axis by axis
  • Champion locked in: runes, spells and item set with “Import all”
02 · Loading Screen

All ten players, before the Rift

A full screen over League's loading screen, with the game plan up top. Rank, form, premades: you know who you're up against before the first second.

  • Team game plan and your personal matchup memo right at the top
  • Premades spelled out: “Duo with Poing”, “Trio with…”
  • Rank, mastery, 12h / 30d activity and tags (“On a roll”, “One-trick”)
  • Ranks show up first, full player cards follow
  • A reminder of your recurring mistake on the champion you're playing, with its tip
03 · In-Game Overlays

Overlays that help you, without playing for you

A compact HUD that lets your clicks through, the Ctrl + X panel and item gaps on TAB. Three presets: Minimal, Standard, Full.

  • Next item adapted to the enemy comp without weakening your champion
  • Ctrl + X: one row per lane, each player facing their opponent
  • Compact HUD: CS/min against your lane opponent, kill participation, vision/min
  • Dragon and Baron timers placed on the minimap
  • Team gold and item gaps while holding TAB
04 · Post-Game Breakdown

A score out of 100, and what explains it

Each stat is compared with the best of the ten players, weighted by your role. A support is judged on vision, not on minions.

  • Role-weighted score out of 100, MVP badge and LP gained or lost
  • What worked, what didn't: strengths and things to work on, with the number behind each
  • Four tabs (Summary, Timeline, Comparison, Players) and a shareable card
  • The moment the game turned, your game phases and your lane duel
  • Your game against your average, and the records you broke

Continuous coaching & LP tracking

The mistake that keeps coming back becomes your priority

RIFU reviews every game and remembers what repeats. You work on one thing at a time, and you see when it's fixed. Everything is compared with your own level, never with other players.

  1. SpottedA mistake that repeats

    Farm dropping after lane, a lead thrown, too much dead time: the post-game flags it when it comes back from one game to the next.

  2. PriorityOne goal, chosen by you

    RIFU suggests the matching goal, for example “1 death at most before 15 min”. You take it, or none.

  3. FixedOne mistake fewer

    Once you stop doing it, it shows as “fixed”. Your strengths are tracked the same way.

  • Your week

    A summary of the past week on the home screen, from 5 games.

  • Per-champion review

    Average score, recurring mistake and strength, from 3 reviewed games.

  • Wins versus losses

    What changes between the two, in your most played role.

  • Progress by axis

    Farming, fighting, vision, survival and objectives, with your 30-day LP curve.

Fair play & Account safety

Helping you play, never playing for you

  1. Official APIs only

    RIFU never reads the game's memory, and only touches League's files to enable replays, when you ask.

  2. Always several options

    Never “play X”. Suggestions come from your pool, not from a single meta.

  3. No hidden advantage

    No MMR estimate, no unmasking of hidden players, no tracking of enemy summoner spells.

  4. Your data stays with you

    Match history, memos and settings stay on your PC. Your feedback is only sent with your consent.

Everything you need to know

Frequently asked questions

Is RIFU free?

Yes, completely. In the long run, RIFU will be funded by discreet ads, only inside the RIFU window and outside games: never in champ select, on the loading screen, in game or in an overlay.

Does RIFU replace Porofessor or DPM?

That's the goal. RIFU brings loading screen scouting, a draft assistant, in-game overlays, a post-game breakdown and LP tracking into one app, with no subscription. Its strength: it explains what your comp is missing and suggests champions from your own pool.

Is the app available in English?

Yes. The app is fully available in English and French. It follows your Windows language, and you can switch it in Settings › Language.

Is my account at risk?

RIFU only uses the interfaces Riot provides to third-party apps: the Riot API, the League client API and the live game data. It never reads the game's memory, only touches League's files to enable replays if you ask, and never plays for you.

It follows Riot's rules for third-party apps: several options rather than a forced pick, no MMR estimate, no unmasking of hidden players in champ select, no tracking of enemy summoner spells or ultimates.

How do I get a beta code?

The beta is closed and invite-only, with one personal code per player. Join the RIFU Discord to ask for a spot.

How is the score out of 100 calculated?

Each stat of the game (kills, kill participation, damage, gold, minions, vision, objectives) is compared with the best of the ten players, then weighted by your role. The best player on the winning team is MVP; the post-game also names the best player on the other team. It is neither a rank nor an MMR estimate.

Do the overlays get in the way?

No. They never take focus from the game, the HUD lets your clicks through, and you set their opacity (30 to 100%) and size. During the beta they need borderless mode; fullscreen will come with the signed version.

Do I need to install Overwolf?

No. RIFU is a standalone app: it doesn't need the Overwolf client.

Why does Windows show a warning?

During the beta the installer isn't signed with a certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen flags it. Signing is planned for the public release. Until then, only download RIFU from this site or the official releases page.

Which modes, servers and systems?

Summoner's Rift (normals, ranked, Co-op vs. AI, custom games), Swiftplay, ARAM and ARAM Mayhem. In Arena, RIFU gives no draft advice, and Brawl games aren't tracked. EUW server, on Windows 10 and 11.

What data is sent?

The minimum needed to show profiles and the loading screen. Everything else stays on your PC, and feedback is only sent if you turn it on. Details are in the privacy policy.
